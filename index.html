How it works

Three calls, one table.

There is no order button here yet — that is the point. This is how a Maggie Lu’s event comes together.

1

Call & tell us the date

Headcount, place, and what kind of table you want. RJ will talk through the menu with you — and roll with the changes.

2

Taste & build your board

A tasting and a menu plan first, so the spread fits your people — not the other way round.

3

We bring it to your table

Boards built, packed, and ready for your wedding, holiday, or work crew — so you can enjoy your own party.
